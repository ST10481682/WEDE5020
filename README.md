#Change Logs 
1. Introduction

I've recently updated the website for Kat's Floral Blossom Bouquet. After looking closely at the original version, I made several improvements. My main goal was to make the site more organized, visually appealing, and super easy for customers to use, all while keeping the same lovely feel of the business.

Tidying Up the Website's Code

I went through and cleaned up a lot of the website's underlying code, fixing several mistakes that were there before.

*   This included sorting out any messy code and making sure everything was properly structured.
*   I removed any unnecessary or repeated bits of code.
*   I also made sure that things like titles, paragraphs, and lists appeared correctly.
*   Finally, I checked that each page had its proper basic setup and that all the page titles were accurate.

Making It Easier to Get Around

I also focused on making it easier for visitors to move between different parts of the website.

  I checked all the menu options for any wrong links or spelling errors, ensuring they guide you to the correct pages.

  The main menu now clearly guides you to important sections like:
    *   Home
    *   About
    *   Services
    *   Bouquets
    *   Occasions
    *   Contact
 Plus, I double-checked all the connections between pages to make sure there are no broken links, so you can browse smoothly without any issues.

4. Website Content Corrections
I've worked on Kat’s Floral Blossom Bouquet website to make it a better and clearer online flower shop. The site now plainly shows what the business offers: fresh flowers, handmade bouquets, custom orders, arrangements for different events, delivery, and contact details. All the information is laid out so visitors can quickly understand the business.

Homepage Improvements: 
The homepage was improved to clearly introduce the business, featuring the headline "Fresh Flowers for Every Moment" and quick details about what Kat’s Floral Blossom Bouquet provides. I also fixed the image sizes and their placement, so they look good and don't take over the entire screen.

About Us Page
The "About Us" page is now simpler, explaining that the business makes handmade bouquets for all kinds of special occasions. The goal was to keep it easy to read and understand the company's purpose.

Services Page
The "Services" page details what customers can get, like fresh arrangements, custom handmade bouquets, and delivery options, including same-day service in some areas. This helps customers know what's available before they even reach out.

Bouquets Section
The "Bouquets" section now clearly displays the different flower arrangements, making it easy for customers to browse and see all the beautiful choices.

Occasions Section
I updated the "Occasions" section to list relevant events like Birthdays, Romance, Graduations, Weddings, and Anniversaries. I removed Mother’s Day and Valentine’s Day, but added Anniversaries as flowers are perfect for celebrating milestones.

Custom Orders & Delivery
There’s now information about custom orders, letting people know they can ask for something unique if they don’t find exactly what they want. Delivery information is also clear, noting available areas and same-day options.

Contact Details
I added full contact details, including the location (Nelspruit/Mbombela), email (info@katsfloral.co.za), and the business's opening hours.

Website Appearance & Usability
I chose a soft, floral colour scheme (blush pink, white, sage green, lavender, cream) to match the flower business. I improved the website's look and feel with better styling. This included making sure all pages were consistent, adjusting image sizes so they fit well and didn't overwhelm the screen, and generally tidying up the layout, text, and buttons. Clear buttons like "Order Now" or "Contact Us" were added to help visitors easily take the next step.

The overall design is now more appealing and consistent, making the site clearly look like a flower business. I also focused on making the website easy to use, so visitors can smoothly find what they need, from learning about the business to checking out bouquets and contacting them.

Final Check
After all changes, I thoroughly checked the site to ensure everything worked correctly and looked good. This included checking pages, navigation, images, text, and buttons, fixing any problems along the way.

In Summary
The main fixes involved correcting technical issues, improving navigation, resizing images, updating all content, and enhancing the design and user flow.

The changes I made have greatly improved Kat’s Floral Blossom Bouquet's website. It now clearly shows what the business offers, is easy to use, and looks much better, providing customers with a smooth experience to explore flowers and services.
